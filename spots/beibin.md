The mouth of the Meilun River (美崙溪) in Hualien, in the lee of the port's west breakwater. The breakwater blocks swell from the north round to the east-southeast, so swell coming straight in from the south-southeast is best. A big northeast swell can still wrap round the breakwater and make waves inside, smaller and softer. The port also shelters it from north and northeast wind.
